    How it plays

    Tap a piece, then tap a glowing square. The pieces are little action figures — they walk square by square like the classic Battle Chess, pull faces when they're picked, panic when they're about to be taken, and duel on captures. Knights hop. Kings and rooks castle properly, pawns can capture en passant and promote.

    You watch from your own side of the board, player POV — your army stands big in front of you, theirs shrinks into the distance. Inspired by the Battle Chess reel — rebuilt from scratch, in colour. No accounts, no servers: everything runs on this page.
